# BiliFast Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

BiliFast is an unofficial, third-party client for Bilibili, made by one developer for personal use. It is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Bilibili.

## What BiliFast collects

Nothing. BiliFast has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data about
you or how you use the app.

## What stays on your device

- Your Bilibili login (cookies and token), kept in the iOS Keychain.
- Settings, search history, recently opened videos, cached pictures and offline downloads, kept in the app's own
  storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
- A diagnostic log, kept on the device. It leaves the device only if you export it yourself, or turn on 发送日志到电脑
  in 开发者, which sends it to a computer on your own local network.

## What goes to Bilibili

As with any Bilibili client, the app talks to Bilibili's own servers to show and play videos, and on your behalf when
you are logged in: watch history, likes, coins, favourites, follows, comments and danmaku you send, and pictures you
attach to a comment. Bilibili's own privacy policy covers that data.

## Permissions

- Photos: only to attach a picture to a comment, or to save a video screenshot you take.
- Camera: only to take a picture for a comment.
- Local network: only for 发送日志到电脑, when you turn it on.

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/KaiC5504/BiliFast/issues.

# 隐私政策（中文）

BiliFast 是非官方的第三方哔哩哔哩客户端，与哔哩哔哩无关。

- **不收集任何数据**：没有服务器、统计、广告或追踪，开发者收不到任何关于你的信息。
- **只存在手机上**：登录信息存在 iOS 钥匙串；设置、搜索历史、图片缓存、离线缓存和诊断日志都存在 App 自己的存储里，删除
  App 即全部删除。日志只有你自己导出，或在「开发者」里打开「发送日志到电脑」（发到你自己局域网里的电脑）时才会离开手机。
- **发给哔哩哔哩的**：和任何 B 站客户端一样，看视频、以及登录后的历史记录、点赞、投币、收藏、关注、评论、弹幕和评论图片，
  都直接发给哔哩哔哩的服务器，适用哔哩哔哩自己的隐私政策。
- **权限**：相册和相机只用于评论配图和保存你截的视频画面；本地网络只用于「发送日志到电脑」。
- **联系**：https://github.com/KaiC5504/BiliFast/issues
